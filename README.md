# CODECRAFT_DS_01

## Task 01 - Data Visualization

### Objective
Create a bar chart to visualize the population distribution of the top 10 most populated countries.

### Dataset
World Bank - World Development Indicators

Indicator:
Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL)

Year:
2025

### Work Performed
- Imported population data from the World Bank dataset.
- Analyzed population data for different countries.
- Identified the top 10 most populated countries.
- Created a bar chart to visualize the population.
- Sorted the countries in descending order of population.

### Top 10 Countries
1. India
2. China
3. United States
4. Indonesia
5. Pakistan
6. Nigeria
7. Brazil
8. Bangladesh
9. Russia
10. Ethiopia

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- World Bank Dataset
- Data Visualization
- Bar Chart

### Output
A bar chart titled:

"Top 10 Most Populated Countries – 2025"
